# Audio credits

All sounds in this folder are **CC0 (public domain)**: free to use in any project, including
commercial ones, with no attribution required. Credit is given here anyway.

| Folder / files | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `weapons/m4_*.wav`, `weapons/ak_*.wav` | Cut from the AR-15 (D_24P, D_32P) and AK-47 (C_28P, C_31P) recordings of *The Free Firearm Sound Library* — https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library | CC0 |
| `footsteps/`, `impacts/`, `weapons/mag_out.ogg`, `weapons/mag_in.ogg`, `weapons/charge.ogg` | Kenney *Impact Sounds* — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | CC0 (see LICENSE_kenney_impact_sounds.txt) |
| `ui/`, `weapons/dry_fire.ogg` | Kenney *Interface Sounds* — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds | CC0 (see LICENSE_kenney_interface_sounds.txt) |

The gunshot clips were made from the original recordings by cutting single shots, mixing to
mono, normalizing and adding a fade-out.
